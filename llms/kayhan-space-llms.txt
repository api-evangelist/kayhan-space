# Kayhan Space — Satcat

> Kayhan Space builds autonomous spaceflight-safety and space-traffic-coordination technology for satellite operators. The Satcat Service API (OpenAPI 3.1, OAuth2 client-credentials) exposes space situational awareness and collision avoidance: conjunction data messages (CDMs), events and mitigation intent, on-orbit screening, ephemeris and TLE catalog services, state vectors and covariance, propagation, orbit determination, maneuver design, launch/LEOP screening, and collision-avoidance (COLA) planning.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the provider's public OpenAPI and docs (no /llms.txt is published by the provider as of 2026-07-19).
